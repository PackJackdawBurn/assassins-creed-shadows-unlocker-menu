## 🧩 About

**Assassins Creed Shadows catalog Menu** is a curated list of Assassins Creed Shadows open-source tools:

- **Overlays** — on-screen helpers, layout notes, map viewer, compass switcher
- **Quality-of-Life Helpers** — gear catalog, cosmetic manager, overlay notes
- **Research Notes** — stealth routes and config notes for educational use

Based on publicly available community tools and overlay helpers.

---

## ✨ Features

### 🎮 Exploration Helpers
- Waypoint Notes — plan routes across any region
- Map Overlay — visualize points of interest and collectibles
- Fast Travel Notes — review unlock states for study
- Playback Speed — adjust menu review for study (0.1x to 5x)

### 🎨 Catalog & Cosmetics
- Gear Catalog — browse weapons, armor, dyes, and trinkets
- Custom Outfit Notes — organize player cosmetics
- Photo Mode Notes — improved capture visibility

### 🏋️ Stealth Layout
- Stealth Mode Notes — segment study on any stronghold
- Season Switch Notes — manually review seasonal states
- Chain Maneuver Notes — study complex traversal in the editor
